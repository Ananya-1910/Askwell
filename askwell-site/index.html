    Before you use any AI tool

    You can study every lesson for free. On Coursera, learners in India and many other countries can apply for financial aid, which can cover the fee.

    Paid certification exams

    These are official, proctored exams that employers recognize. Prices are in US dollars and are charged in your local currency, plus tax, when you book.

    Good to know

    A free certificate of completion shows you finished a course. A paid certification exam is proctored and tests what you know, so employers usually value it more. Start with the free courses, then take an exam when you feel ready. Prices and rules change, so always confirm on the official page before you pay.
